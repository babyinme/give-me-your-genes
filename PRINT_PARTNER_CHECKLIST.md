# MiniMeMagic print partner checklist

Status: planning only; no quotes or suppliers confirmed.

Get three written quotes for baby figurines, character keychains, family sets and magnetic stands. Ask for quantity breaks at 1, 10, 50 and 100 units, manufacturing process, material, color, finishing, packaging, shipping to Alaska and the lower 48, lead time, reprint policy, confidentiality, model ownership, and order tracking.

Margin per order = selling price minus manufacturing, model preparation, AI, payment fees, packaging, shipping, returns allowance and customer acquisition. Price against outsourced costs even when producing samples in-house.

Before accepting orders: test samples from at least two partners, verify printable STL/3MF geometry, confirm product quality and consumer safety, approve policies and payment workflow, and establish minimum contribution margin.

Example only: $79 revenue minus $25 production, $8 shipping, $4 AI and processing, $7 modeling, $5 reprint allowance and $12 acquisition leaves $18 contribution. No real vendor prices have been verified.
