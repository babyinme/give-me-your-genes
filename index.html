Good to Know

Does this predict what our real baby would look like?

No. Results will be fictional creative images, not genetic, medical, or scientific predictions.

Are my photos uploaded when I try the demo?

Photos are previewed locally first. If you choose to generate a baby, the photos are sent to our server and an external AI image provider for processing. Do not submit photos without consent.

Can I order a 3D printed character today?

Not yet. We're developing the product and ordering experience.
